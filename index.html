Data coverage & caveats

  1. Likes per user are unavailable from the X API (get_posts_liking_users returns 403 under app-only auth); likes appear only as per-post totals. Per-user “engagers” therefore cover replies, quotes, mentions and reposts.
  2. Team accounts are excluded from all outside counts: @MavrykIntern, @doxxed_mavryk, @mv1_Davis, @theSamMorrow, @trimavryk (and @MavrykNetwork itself).
  3. Reposters were fetched only for 26 top posts; repost-only engagers of other posts are missed.
  4. Inbound replies/quotes/mentions coverage: Jul 10 12:06 – Oct 22 08:49 IST (804 records). Windows run back from the data as-of time (Oct 22 2026, 08:30 IST). The first weekly bucket may be partial.
  5. Recent posts are still accruing impressions/engagements, so the latest week is understated.
  6. Judgment calls (labelled): sentiment (keyword heuristics + inline tags), format/topic labels, account categories and ‘why it matters’ notes, and question/complaint tags. A July bot cluster (~100 templated replies from 11 accounts) inflates July spam share.

Reach % = average impressions per sampled post ÷ followers. Mavryk’s eng/post & reach use our own last-30d posts.
